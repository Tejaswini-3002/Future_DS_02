Overview:
This project was completed as part of the Future Interns Data Analytics Internship Program.
The objective of this project was to analyze telecom customer data and build an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides insights into customer behavior, churn trends, and key business metrics. The dashboard helps visualize customer retention patterns and supports data-driven decision-making.

Objectives:
* Analyze customer churn trends
* Monitor customer retention metrics
* Evaluate the revenue impact of churn
* Understand customer demographics and behavior
* Explore churn patterns across services and contract types
* Generate actionable business insights through data visualization

Tools Used:
* Power BI
* DAX
* Data Cleaning & Transformation
* Data Visualization

Dashboard Features:
* Provides a comprehensive overview of total customers, churned customers, churn rate, and revenue-related KPIs.
* Analyzes customer churn across different contract types, payment methods, and internet services.
* Visualizes customer demographics, including gender and senior citizen distribution, to identify behavioral patterns.
* Tracks customer tenure and monthly charges to understand customer value and retention trends.
* Highlights revenue impact and customer attrition through interactive charts and KPI cards.
* Includes dynamic filters and slicers that allow users to explore churn trends and customer segments interactively.

Key Insights:
* Identified customer segments with higher churn rates.
* Analyzed the relationship between contract types and customer retention.
* Evaluated the impact of telecom services on customer churn.
* Explored revenue implications associated with customer attrition.
* Visualized customer demographics and behavior patterns.

Outcome:
This dashboard demonstrates how customer data can be transformed into meaningful insights that help businesses monitor performance, understand customer behavior, and support retention-focused strategies.
